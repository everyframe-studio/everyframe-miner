# everycli v1.3.4

Miners can now serve Sand.ai MAGI-2 through WaveSpeedAI, Kie.ai, NanoAPI and Revana AI, in addition to Higgsfield. The new routes support 4–30 second videos in 1080p with audio, text prompts, opening and closing frames, and image references. WaveSpeedAI, Kie.ai and NanoAPI also support video and audio references.

Run `everycli update` and reopen the dashboard if it is running. To serve the new routes, run `everycli worker-update` from your existing profile, configure the provider credentials, and enable its MAGI-2 offer in Models.
