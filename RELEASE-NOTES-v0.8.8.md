## Easier model and provider offers

- New model offers default to 0% discount. Zero-percent and inactive offers use a neutral color.
- The **Not offered** tab shows models without an active offer. Model labels, provider counts and the overview link open that filtered view directly.
- Provider switches turn offers on or off together. Turning on keeps saved discounts and uses 0% for new models; turning off withdraws offers and keeps their discounts for later.
- Provider changes use one reviewed batch. Existing active offers stay unchanged when turning a provider on, and changed prices or revisions require a fresh review.

## Update

```sh
everycli update
```

Close and reopen `everycli ui` to load the new controls. No worker update is required.
