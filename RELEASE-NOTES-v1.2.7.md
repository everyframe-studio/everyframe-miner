# everycli v1.2.7

- Adds direct Vidu Q3 Turbo for text, image, and first/last-frame video, plus Vidu Q4 Preview image-to-video through 4K.
- Adds direct Stability AI Stable Image Core, Stable Image Ultra, and Stable Audio 2.5.
- Adds seven WaveSpeedAI routes for Ideogram 4.6, MiniMax H3 Singularity, and Seedance 2.5 Flash. WaveSpeedAI upscales Flash video at 1080p and 4K.

Run `everycli update` and reopen `everycli ui` if it is running. For Vidu or Stability AI, use `everycli worker-update --check` to check for a compatible signed worker. Once installed, apply the provider key and review the new offers in Models. Compatible WaveSpeedAI workers receive catalog additions after coordinator rollout.
