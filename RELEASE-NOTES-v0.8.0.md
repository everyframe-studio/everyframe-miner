## Director tools

Miners can offer discounts for the new Director tools: Kling 4.0 Edit, H3 Max Recast and Relight, VEED Dubbing, HeyGen Voice, FLUX Klein LoRA, and Motion-to-Code.

The worker checks referenced files before submission and returns Motion-to-Code's source files together with its video preview.

Update your CLI with `everycli update`. If your worker needs a manual update, run `everycli worker-update --check` and follow the instructions it shows.
